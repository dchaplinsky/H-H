How to use this
  • Go in order within a part — modules build on each other, exactly as the book's chapters do. Parts II onward assume Part I is solid.
  • Play with every interactive until the behaviour stops surprising you. The goal is intuition you can apply while reading a schematic, not memorized formulas.
  • Do the design bench tasks on paper first, then check the solution. Designing (even badly) beats reading about designing.
  • Pair each module with the corresponding book sections listed in its header — the modules distill; the book has the depth.
